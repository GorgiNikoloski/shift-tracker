Shift Pay Tracker

Tap a day to assign a shift or mark a public holiday. Sundays are tinted.

Under the Labour Relations Law (Art. 132) the daily break counts as working time and is paid, so breaks are paid by default. Sunday and holiday bonuses follow the real calendar date of each hour, so a shift crossing midnight changes day at 00:00. To pay a holiday bonus for hours after midnight, mark the holiday on its own date, even if you have no shift starting that day. Bonuses stack additively. Example: night + holiday = 35% + 250% = 285% on top of base pay. Salary shown is gross: hours × rate + bonuses.

Data is saved automatically in this browser. Use export to move it to another device. Importing replaces current data.
